One line, at most 160 characters: a weather forecast for a city that does
not exist, read deadpan like a real TV broadcast — written for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-ibis-18-deadpan-forecasts-for-cities-that-do-151d4d8c).

`main.ts` writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`). The line names the invented city of
Brindlewick, gives its weather with concrete numbers (rain falling sideways
at 47 degrees, high of 14, low of 13), and lands the deadpan twist in the
last clause: the mayor has already apologised for the damp, as he does every
Tuesday — absurd, yet delivered exactly like a straight-faced forecaster
would say it.
